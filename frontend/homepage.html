How GramBandhan Works

Our simple process connects investors with rural entrepreneurs for transparent, ethical growth

1

Farmers Create Projects

Verified rural entrepreneurs list their detailed agricultural or artisan business plans for the community.

2

Investors Fund Ventures

Choose from diverse bonds including crops, livestock, or handicrafts, and invest with as little as 5,000 BDT.

3

Training & Support

Admin provides continuous training to farmers and artisans, helping them improve skills and work effectively using their expertise.

4

Production & Monitoring

With guidance and resources in place, production begins while admin monitors progress and ensures quality standards are maintained.

5

Sales & Distribution

Admin collects and sells goods through online platform and local markets. Customers can also purchase directly from the platform.

6

Profit Distribution

Generated profit is distributed fairly between investors as returns, farmers and artisans as income, and the platform for operational support.

GramBandhan operates an asset-backed, direct participatory model. You select vetted agricultural cycles (such as Bogura Potato, Boro Rice in Sylhet, or Manikganj Mustard) or rural artisanal cooperatives across 18 districts of Bangladesh.

  • Vetted Projects: Each project is inspected, soil-tested, and audited before listing.
  • Digital Funding: Invest securely through bKash, Nagad, or standard Bangladeshi bank transfers (BEFTN/NPSB).
  • Seasonal Payouts: Upon harvest and wholesale distribution, principal capital and contractual profit-share are deposited into your wallet.
100% Tangible Asset Backing: Capital directly funds verified seeds, organic fertilizers, and farming inputs.

Agriculture carries natural climate factors. GramBandhan deploys a comprehensive four-layer risk mitigation shield:

  • GNSS Satellite & Telemetry: Real-time satellite NDVI crop-health analysis and localized ground moisture sensors flag anomalies days before visible symptoms appear.
  • Parametric Micro-Takaful: Cooperative Islamic insurance shields farmer cohorts against catastrophic floods, cyclonic surges, or severe localized drought.
  • Agronomic Supervision: Dedicated field agronomists stationed in every district inspect plots weekly and enforce scientific disease management.
  • Geographic Diversification: Portfolios spread investments across diverse upazilas to avoid single-zone weather shocks.
High-Tech Defense: Real-time satellite radar and ground IoT telemetry protect crop vitality 24/7.

We prioritize genuine smallholders and marginalized female artisans who lack access to institutional banking:

  • On-Ground Verification: Our district coordinators verify land ownership, Union Parishad identity credentials, and past 3-year farming track record.
  • Inputs Instead of Cash: We provide certified high-germination seeds, organic fertilizers, and mechanized tools rather than unsecured cash loans.
  • Eliminating Middlemen (Farias): Direct harvest procurement links farmers directly to corporate buyers and our consumer marketplace, delivering 70%+ higher net earnings.
Social Impact: Over 1,200 smallholder families and 350+ rural women artisans sustainably empowered.
